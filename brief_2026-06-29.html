$343M One South Wacker loan defaults on maturity

601W Companies failed to repay a $343M Blackstone Mortgage Trust loan on One South Wacker Drive — Chicago's 40-story West Loop tower — when it matured June 9. The building is 73% leased; this is a refinancing failure, not an operational one.

Why it matters

One of the largest named Chicago office maturity defaults of 2026, feeding directly into the $76.6B of CMBS loans facing hard deadlines this year.

By the numbers
  • $343M loan | 40 stories | 73% occupied | 2018 origination
  • On Blackstone Mortgage Trust watchlist since 2022 | <2% of BXMT portfolio
  • Two blocks from Willis Tower — West Loop has outperformed other downtown submarkets
Between the lines

The property still cash flows — this is a rate-gap default, not an income default. The 2018 loan can't be refinanced at current all-in rates without a large equity injection the sponsor isn't providing.

What's next

Special servicer assumes control. Expect appraisal reduction, cash trap activation, and an extension-vs.-foreclosure standoff over the next 6–12 months.

For dummies

The owner couldn't find a new lender to pay off the old loan at maturity, so the special servicer now runs the resolution. Classic 2026 maturity-wall mechanics — full fee-generating workout incoming, but office values make a clean exit hard.

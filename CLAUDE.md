# cmbs-briefs

Daily CMBS/CRE morning brief for an Associate in Asset Management at CWCapital Asset Management.

## Delivery

Do NOT attempt to send or draft the brief via the Microsoft 365 / Outlook connector.
That was tried and the connector's Entra app registration lacks admin-consented
Mail.Send / Mail.ReadWrite permissions (403 ErrorAccessDenied on both send and draft).

Instead: just output the finished brief directly in the chat response. Optionally
save it as `<YYYY-MM-DD>.html` in this repo and commit/push it as a record, but
chat output is the primary delivery — no email step needed.

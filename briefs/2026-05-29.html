CMBS maturity wall cresting — Q4 is the danger zone

Trepp’s Spring 2026 Quarterly Data Review puts hard CMBS maturities at $76.6B this year, with 39% (~$29.9B) concentrated in Q4. Of all loans facing maturity, 36% carry debt yields at or below 8% — refinancing at current rates is nearly impossible without a principal haircut.

  • ~$25B in CMBS loans are past maturity with no resolution — no repayment, no liquidation, no formal extension.
  • Office CMBS delinquency peaked at a record 12.34% in January before pulling back to 11.20% in February; still the most distressed major property type.
  • CMBS special servicing rate: 10.91% (January), with office driving 59% of new transfers ($1.33B of $2.3B that month).

Why it matters: The Q4 maturity spike is a direct workload signal — a surge of new SS transfers, consent cases, and hard maturity extensions is loading onto Q3 action calendars now.

Sources: Trepp Spring 2026 Quarterly Data Review; CRE Daily; REI Prime

For dummies: A massive pile of CMBS loans comes due this year, with the heaviest load hitting October–December. Most can’t refinance at today’s rates — which means they’re headed to special servicers like CWCAM.

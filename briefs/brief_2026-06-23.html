June maturity wall breaks: $313M mall transfers to special servicing

The $312.6M CMBS loan on Lakewood Center mall transferred to special servicing last week for imminent maturity default — one of the largest retail loan casualties as the June 2026 maturity wave crests.

  • Why it matters: June was always circled as peak-maturity month on the 2026 wall. This is the front edge of expected workout volume landing on loan-level in real time.
  • By the numbers: CMBS overall distress rate hit 12.07% in March (CRED iQ), a new cycle high. SS rate stands at 8.23% in June; retail at 10.82% and office at 10.79%.
  • Between the lines: At 10.82%, retail SS has quietly matched office — watch for mall and power-center loans to dominate new workout volume in Q3.
For dummies: A giant mall loan couldn't refinance when it came due, so it just landed on a servicer's workout desk. Expect a flood of similar handoffs through year-end as hundreds of billions of 2021-vintage loans mature.

Source: CRED iQ, Commercial Real Estate Direct, Bisnow

Blackstone's Burbank office walks to SS at maturity

An $87M CMBS loan backed by a Burbank, CA office building co-owned by Blackstone and Worthe Real Estate Group transferred to special servicing for imminent monetary default. The loan matured; the borrower has not provided a plan.

  • Between the lines: No update from borrower = foreclosure is a live option. Burbank office vacancy remains elevated post-media-industry contraction.
  • Yes but: Blackstone's overall RE platform is healthy — this is a surgical exit on an underwater California office, not a balance sheet crisis.
For dummies: Even a mega-sponsor like Blackstone is handing back the keys on a low-occupancy California office when the math doesn't work — watch for more strategic walk-aways as maturity deadlines hit.

Source: Bisnow

Capital markets open; issuance up 43% YTD

YTD CMBS issuance stands at $38.4B — up 43% vs. same-period 2025. KBRA rated the BBCMS 2026-5C42 ($633.5M conduit) last week. Conduit AAA spreads hold at +78bp; BBB- at +450bp. SASB AAA ranges +108–177bp by property type.

  • Driving the news: SASB issuance now exceeds conduit; Tishman Speyer's $2.65B Spiral tower refi (SASB) set the tone for high-quality collateral execution.
  • The bottom line: The +372bp AAA-to-BBB- gap signals the market is charging a steep premium for junior risk — even as deal flow is robust at the top of the stack.
For dummies: New CMBS deals are pricing easily for trophy collateral, but the bond market is demanding much higher yields to take on riskier slices — a sign that distress is priced in at the bottom, not ignored.

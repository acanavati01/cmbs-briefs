1 big thing: CIM defaults on Kaiser's Oakland tower

CIM Group got a default notice on the $97.1M loan backing Kaiser Permanente's Oakland HQ at 1 Kaiser Plaza — the property's value is now near a quarter of its 2016 peak.

  • Loan went to special servicing in Oct. 2024 after Kaiser cut its footprint by a third, pushing cash flow to breakeven
  • CIM says in SEC filings it's negotiating a long-term resolution or extension with the lender/special servicer
  • Sister asset Central Tower is separately headed to foreclosure

Why it matters: Oakland's office market (~33% vacant) is resetting fast — CIM's twin defaults show even blue-chip, single-tenant-anchored offices aren't safe from maturity distress.

For dummies: A big landlord can't repay its office loan because its main tenant shrank and rent no longer covers debt — expect a workout, paydown, or foreclosure, and a preview of what's coming for other single-tenant offices.

Source: The Real Deal

Harborplace back in special servicing

The CMBS loan on Baltimore's Harborplace (UBSBB 2013-C5) transferred to Midland again ahead of next month's maturity, unlikely to be paid off.

For dummies: This mall loan has been here before and is heading there again — redevelopment-stage retail collateral rarely resolves on the first try.

Source: Commercial Real Estate Direct

Yields climb, FOMC in blackout

The 10-year Treasury rose to 4.63% on July 21 as US-Iran tensions escalated for a tenth day. The Fed is expected to hold rates next week, but markets price ~68% odds of a September hike.

For dummies: Higher, stickier rates make refinancing maturing CMBS loans harder — bad news for anything already on a special servicer's watchlist.

1 big thing

Warsh's first FOMC shifts dot plot toward hikes — refi math gets harder

The June 17 FOMC held the fed funds rate at 4.25–4.50%, but the message underneath was hawkish: 9 of 18 committee members now project at least one hike before year-end. The median 2026 year-end rate estimate jumped to 3.8% from 3.4% in March. PCE inflation was revised up to 3.6%. New Chair Kevin Warsh notably declined to submit his own dot.

SOFR 3.63%  |  2Y ~4.29%  |  10Y 4.46%  |  30Y 4.90%  |  Fed funds 4.25–4.50%

  • Why it matters: The 10Y at 4.46% and 30Y at 4.90% compress exit cap rates and blow out refi spreads — directly worsening resolution math on the $875B CRE maturity wall still queued for 2026.
  • Between the lines: Warsh's absent dot is strategically ambiguous. Markets can't anchor his bias; rate-sensitive borrowers needing extensions or mods are left in limbo.
  • Yes, but: Six of the nine projected hike-dots are for just one 25bp move. A single hike is a known risk; what spooked the bond market was the PCE revision.

Sources: CNBC, CNN Business, BondSavvy, Chase.com — June 17, 2026

For dummies: The Fed just told markets rates could go up, not down — meaning every CMBS borrower trying to refi or extend a troubled loan just got a worse hand to play at the negotiating table.

Distressed workout

20 Times Square misses maturity — back in special servicing, again

Maefield Development's $647.5M CMBS loan (Times Square Trust 2018-20TS, Natixis origination) transferred to special servicing after missing its May 2026 maturity date — the second SS stint for this asset. It first entered SS in November 2022, secured an extension in late 2023, and has now blown through that deadline too.

  • Driving the news: The 454-key Edition by Marriott hotel and the retail podium have chronically underperformed original underwriting assumptions.
  • Yes, but: Single-borrower SASB structure (2018-20TS) gives the servicer wide resolution flexibility — but also gives Maefield maximum leverage to drag.
  • What's next: Servicer must choose among note sale, deed-in-lieu, foreclosure, or a second modification — each carrying meaningful timeline and recovery implications.

Sources: Commercial Observer, Hoodline — May/June 2026

For dummies: A $647M Times Square hotel/retail loan landed on a servicer's desk for the second time — the 2023 extension bought two years but solved nothing, so now it's modify again, force a sale, or foreclose.

  • By the numbers: BNK52 AAA (Class A-1) priced at +70 J-spread. Pool WA LTV: 48.7% — very conservative underwriting at current rates.
  • Yes, but: $2.9B in new office/retail loans transferred to SS in May. The headline rate improvement masks continued inflows; easy resolutions are thinning.

Sources: CRE News Direct, SEC EDGAR, Morningstar DBRS, Trepp / CRE Daily — June 2026

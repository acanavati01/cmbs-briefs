1 big thing
S2 Capital's CMBS book implodes — $250M flagged for SS

Dallas multifamily operator S2 Capital had $250M in CMBS loans transferred to special servicing in late May, as its rate-cap-funded REIT structure collapsed under floating-rate stress.

  • Driving the news: The Republic Apartments (Garland, TX — 1,033 units) faces a June foreclosure auction on a $78.6M loan from Benefit Street Partners (2021 vintage); Scott Everett says property is under contract for sale.
  • By the numbers: Feeder fund Trinity Investors has warned LPs to expect a full wipeout of capital in S2's REIT.
  • Between the lines: Loans span multiple conduit pools — each with its own PSA, modification authority, and appraisal-reduction trigger clock.
For dummies: A big Dallas apartment landlord can't pay its CMBS debt; loan trustees are taking control across multiple bond deals, and the investors who backed the REIT have been told their equity is worthless.
Sources: The Real Deal Texas, May 27–28; Multifamily Dive
Retail distress
SoCal mall's $309M CMBS note hits workout table

The Pacific Retail/Lyon/Silverpeak JV that bought Lakewood Center from Macerich in summer 2025 for $332M is in active modification talks after the loan (DBWF 2015-LCN) moved to SS in January 2026.

  • Yes, but: No missed monthly payments; borrower requesting extension + parcel sales to reduce refi risk.
  • Ratings: S&P and DBRS Morningstar have downgraded multiple classes of DBWF 2015-LCN.
For dummies: New mall owners bought with barely any equity above the debt and now need to sell off pieces of the parking lot just to refinance — bondholders are getting paid to wait, nervously.
Source: The Real Deal LA, June 4, 2026; Morningstar DBRS
Macro distress
Texas CRE foreclosures hit record $1.3B in June

Loans flagged for June auction in the Texas Triangle hit a new high since Real Deal began tracking in May 2025, with Dallas County leading at 11 of 48 loans.

  • Property mix is diversifying: Dallas hotels, 6 Wash Guys Car Wash locations, and Nitya Capital's Palace Apartments (Fort Worth, $28.2M) all on the block.
  • May 2026: >$1.0B → June 2026: $1.3B (record)
For dummies: Texas commercial foreclosure auctions just broke a record — and it's no longer just apartments; hotels and small businesses are now also failing to make their loan payments.
Source: The Real Deal Texas, June 1, 2026
Capital markets
BBCMS 2026-5C42: $633.5M conduit priced

Barclays/Deutsche Bank priced a $633.5M conduit deal (37 loans, 58 props, 15 MSAs) around June 2; settlement June 24. LNR Partners named SS; Midland as master servicer.

  • What's next: KBRA becomes the 4th agency added to Bloomberg's CMBS index methodology, effective June 2026 month-end rebalance — watch for spread recalibration.
For dummies: New CMBS deals are still getting done at scale and investors are still buying, even as older loans keep defaulting — issuance and distress are running in parallel.
Sources: SEC EDGAR BBCMS 2026-5C42 8-K/FWP; KBRA; Bloomberg Index Services
